# Changelog

## 1.4.0

- **Talk to the garrison commander** on the main castle market menu for covered castles when a lord's **Needs Garrison Troops** quest targets that castle. Vanilla towns already list this on the main town menu; vanilla castles only had it on **Approach the gates** (`castle_guard`), which CMT's Direct walk-in skips. Uses the base game's own option text and logic. Uncovered castles are unchanged.
- **Deliver a Herd** at a refused castle market gate. When **Deliver a Herd (village)** is on and you have an active delivery to that castle, the base game's **entry denied** screen (the public gate used for the castle market, not **Go to the castle**) gains **Deliver Herd to Guards** beside **Continue**. The guards offer to take the herd to the buyer for half the quest gold; **Accept** finishes the quest with that reduced reward, **Reject** returns to the denied screen, and **Continue** still leaves as before. Apply-to-kingdom / NPC and other coverage switches do not gate this option: it follows the active quest and the Deliver a Herd setting.
- **Artisan Can't Sell Products** can deliver to a covered castle (as well as a town). When that quest is on and the destination castle market gate is blocked, **Deliver Items to Guards** works like the herd option: half reward, product name in the offer text, same denial / disguise / FaceCharges menus. Translation IDs: `cmt_artisan_deliver_to_guards`, `cmt_artisan_guard_offer`, `cmt_artisan_guard_accept`, `cmt_artisan_guard_reject`, `cmt_artisan_guard_need_items`, `cmt_artisan_guard_products`; changed text: `cmt_mcm_quest_artisan_cant_sell_hint`, `cmt_mcm_quest_deliver_herd_hint`.
- If a castle **loses CMT coverage**, active **Castle quests** tied to it (every toggled quest: escort, brigands, overpriced goods, artisan can't sell, caravan ambush, army of poachers, inn and out, deliver a herd, family feud) are **cancelled softly** — journal note on quests and open/alternative issues, no relation / fail penalties; for **Deliver a Herd** you also keep the animals (and the animal count is restored if cancel fails). Village-bound quests such as **Family Feud** are matched via the castle's bound villages. One failed cancel no longer skips the rest; leftover failures are retried on later cleanup. Turning off only a single quest MCM toggle does **not** cancel ongoing quests (the castle still has notables).
- Covered castles use **town-like gate access** for war and crime, with castle wording where it matters; bad relation with the lord stays a hard refuse (checked before any crime conversion). **At war:** sneak through the castle gate (same % / catch risk as towns), approach to meet someone (request entry hidden while sneak is up), besiege, leave. Disguise success lands in the **castle** menu; leave / fail / escape return to **castle_outside** (not town menus). **Crime:** same faction moderate/severe → FaceCharges is offered **before** a direct walk-in can switch to `castle` (`town_inside_criminal`, already castle-aware); neutral moderate/severe → LimitedAccess disguise like a town; mild crime no longer hard-blocks as vanilla castles do. **Bad relation:** NoAccess denial menu. **Welcome:** market-gate walk-in; keep under **Go to the castle**. **Deliver Herd to Guards** / **Deliver Items to Guards** (half reward) appear only while entry is blocked (denial, disguise, or FaceCharges), not when the gate is open, when those quests target the castle. Translation IDs: `cmt_castle_disguise`, `cmt_herd_deliver_to_guards`, `cmt_herd_guard_offer`, `cmt_herd_guard_accept`, `cmt_herd_guard_reject`, `cmt_herd_guard_need_animals`, `cmt_artisan_deliver_to_guards`, `cmt_artisan_guard_offer`, `cmt_artisan_guard_accept`, `cmt_artisan_guard_reject`, `cmt_artisan_guard_need_items`, `cmt_artisan_guard_products`, `cmt_quest_cancel_coverage`; changed text: `cmt_mcm_quest_deliver_herd_hint`, `cmt_mcm_quest_artisan_cant_sell_hint`.

## 1.3.3

This release includes everything since 1.2.0. It replaces 1.3.2, which was withdrawn.

- Castle training. **Go to the castle** has a new **Visit the castle trainers** option in castles this mod covers. You and the adult companions and family members of your clan travelling in your party can **Train martial skills** (One Handed, Two Handed, Polearm, Bow, Crossbow, Throwing, Riding, Athletics, Scouting, Smithing) or **Study administrative skills** (Leadership, Tactics, Steward, Trade, Engineering, Charm, Roguery, Medicine). One session costs 1,000 gold and 10 influence, whoever trains, and each hero gains 2 to 10 levels in up to 3 random skills of the group that are below 150, and no skill goes above 150. Levels are added directly: learning limits and focus points do not reduce them, and they give no character experience, so heroes do not level up from training. **Train an attribute** raises one attribute of one hero by 1 for 5,000 gold and 25 influence, never above the game's maximum. A trained hero waits 7 days before training skills again, and separately 7 days before training another attribute. The cost is checked before anything is paid, the result lists what each hero gained, and the trainers are closed during a siege. Everything is set in the new **Castle training** group in MCM, whose switch turns the whole feature on or off (on by default): costs, skills per session, the gain range, the skill cap, both cooldowns (skills 2 to 15 days, attributes 5 to 20) and who may train. Training changes are permanent character progression and remain in the save if the mod is disabled or removed.
- Castle market prices work like town prices. The castle **Trade** screen used to price every item at its flat base value, whether buying or selling, because the base game's trade screen only reads the markets of towns and villages. It now uses the castle's own market, so prices follow the castle's supply and demand, change as you buy and sell in bulk, and include the same difference between buying and selling prices as in towns. The castle market settings in MCM, such as starting stock and production, now affect the prices you see. Caravans and AI lords already used the castle's own market and are unchanged.
- The Trade perk **Trickle Down** also works in castles this mod covers: buying trade goods worth 10,000 or more improves your relation with the castle's merchants, as in towns.
- Town and castle workshops have separate limits. Towns keep the base game's limit of clan tier + 1, raised by **Extra town workshop limit**. Castles get their own limit of clan tier + 1, raised by the new **Extra castle workshop limit** (People and workshops, 0 to 10, default 0). Each limit counts only its own workshops, so castle workshops no longer take up town slots. The castle limit is worked out on its own, so mods or edits that change the town workshop limit do not change it. Buying a workshop from a notable checks the matching limit and says which one is reached, and the clan screen's **Other** tab shows both, for example "Workshops (towns 2/3, castles 1/3)". **Extra workshop limit** is renamed **Extra town workshop limit** and keeps its saved value.
- New MCM settings in **People and workshops** for the castle limit: **Fixed castle workshop limit** (off by default) uses **Castle workshop limit** (1 to 100, default 10) instead of clan tier + 1, whatever your clan tier, and **Unlimited castle workshops** (off by default) removes the castle limit. The clan screen shows "unlimited" for castles when it is on.
- **Workshops** is renamed **Castle workshop slots**, since it only sets the slots in each castle. It keeps its saved value, now goes up to 8 and defaults to 2 instead of 1.
- New MCM setting **Town workshop slots** (People and workshops, 4 to 10, default 4). Each slot above the base game's 4 adds a workshop to every town, run by the town's notables and bought and managed like any other, though it has no building or sign in the town scene. Lowering it removes only those added workshops, never the base game's 4 or one owned by you or a lord. At 4, towns are untouched.
- Only adults without a clan are sent to castle taverns. Unhired companions of the base game and of companion overhaul mods normally are, so their moves are unchanged. Children and clan members that another mod gives the wanderer occupation, such as They Married's children living with their caretakers in towns, are no longer sent to castles. Any already in a castle can still be sent back to a town as before. The one-time castle seed checks the same: if a replacement companion system creates a child or a clan member, that hero goes to a nearby town and seeding stops.
- CompanionsCraft support, without requiring it or changing its files. Leaving a castle with companions in your party no longer crashes the game when the castle market stocks hardwood, charcoal, iron ore or iron. CompanionsCraft's companions now buy those materials in castles at the castle market's prices, as they do in towns. Without CompanionsCraft nothing is patched.
- They Married support, without requiring it or changing its files. When you send a companion to a clan whose home is a castle this mod covers, and **Recruit troops in castles** is on, the new lord's starting troops include recruits from the castle's merchants and artisans, as they already do from town and village notables. Without They Married nothing is patched.
- Translation support. All of the mod's own text, including the MCM page title and every group, setting name and hint, the castle menus and castle training, the workshop limit messages, the castle workshop sale notification and the error messages, now comes from `ModuleData/Languages/EN/cmt_strings_en.xml`. The mod still ships in English; anyone may publish a translation as a separate mod that requires Castle Market Towns, without changing its files. [TRANSLATORS.md](TRANSLATORS.md) explains how. Castle menu lines taken from the base game continue to use the game's own translations.
- Translation IDs introduced in this version: `cmt_mcm_title`, the `cmt_mcm_group_*` group names, a `cmt_mcm_*_name` and `cmt_mcm_*_hint` pair for every setting, the `cmt_menu_*` castle menu lines, the `cmt_training_*` castle training texts, `cmt_workshop_town_limit_reached`, `cmt_workshop_castle_limit_reached`, `cmt_clan_workshop_counts`, `cmt_workshop_limit_unlimited`, `cmt_notice_workshops_sold`, `cmt_error_ransom_failed`, `cmt_error_hot_butter_tavern` and `cmt_error_tavern_open`. The earlier `cmtTavern01` to `cmtTavern03`, `cmtKeep01` and `cmtWorkshop01` IDs were renamed to these.
- MCM tooltips are shorter and easier to read.
- The README now names Castle Redux as not supported alongside this mod, and explains why: its extra notables put village headmen in castles, where the base game's village quests fail.
- Apart from the renamed workshop settings above, saved MCM values carry over unchanged.
- **Coming from 1.3.2:** new are the castle market prices, **Trickle Down** in castles, the castle workshop limit that no longer follows the town limit, and the **Fixed castle workshop limit**, **Castle workshop limit** and **Unlimited castle workshops** settings. Translation IDs added since 1.3.2: `cmt_mcm_fixed_castle_workshop_limit_name`, `cmt_mcm_fixed_castle_workshop_limit_hint`, `cmt_mcm_castle_workshop_max_name`, `cmt_mcm_castle_workshop_max_hint`, `cmt_mcm_unlimited_castle_workshops_name`, `cmt_mcm_unlimited_castle_workshops_hint` and `cmt_workshop_limit_unlimited`; changed text: `cmt_mcm_castle_workshop_limit_hint`.

## 1.2.0

- Workshops you own in castles appear in the clan screen's **Other** tab next to your town workshops, with the same status, capital and wage details, warehouse settings (**Get Input from the Warehouse** and **Store Outputs in the Warehouse**) and **Manage Workshop** options to change production or sell. Before, they were counted toward your workshop limit but never listed.
- Castles where you own a workshop get **Enter the warehouse** in the castle menu, the same warehouse screen and capacity as in towns.
- When a castle stops being covered, because **Enable castle markets** or an **Apply to** switch is turned off or the castle passes to a clan those switches leave out, workshops you own there are sold to one of its notables at the base game's price before the notables leave, or to a notable of the nearest town if none is left. Their warehouse goods go to your party if you are in the castle, to the castle stash if your clan owns it, and are otherwise sold at the castle's price. Before, those workshops stayed yours but stopped running and could not be sold.
- A notable's tooltip lists the workshops they own in castles as well as in towns.
- New MCM setting **Extra workshop limit** (People and workshops, 0 to 10, default 0). It adds to the base game's limit of clan tier + 1 workshops, which castle and town workshops share. Raising it mid-campaign takes effect at once, warehouses included.
- Improved Garrisons support, without requiring it or changing its files. In covered castles its button moves into **Go to the castle**, after the dungeon, as it sits in **Go to the keep** in towns. Its project tooltips show the castle Marketplace tariff and the Castellan's Office tax. Its recruiter, patrol and mobile garrison parties treat covered castles like towns: they recruit from castle merchants and artisans, sell loot and buy horses in the castle market. New MCM switch **Improved Garrisons support** (General, on by default) turns all three off. Without Improved Garrisons nothing is patched.
- A castle Marketplace that was queued for construction after the castle stopped being covered is taken out of the queue the next day.
- AI lords sell their loot in castle markets and plan trips to castles to buy food, as they do with towns. They only head to a castle for food when it has some to spare above the castle's own reserve, and judge the trip by that spare food alone, not the whole stock.
- Lords far from their own lands consider covered castles of other kingdoms as places to visit, as they already did with neutral towns and villages.
- When a castle workshop changes hands, through bankruptcy, its owner's death, a sale or war, and the base game finds no buyer in the castle, any other notable of the castle takes it. With none left, a bankrupt owner keeps it with fresh capital; otherwise a notable of the nearest town takes it over until the castle has merchants or artisans again, and it returns to one of them on the castle's next daily update, or is removed with the other mod workshops if the castle is no longer covered. Before, this could throw an error with only one notable in the castle.
- Mod workshops left in a castle that stops being covered, because nobody could buy them, keep producing and paying wages. Workshops another mod added there are left to that mod.
- Caravans trading at castles count toward trade agreements, and trade rumours and the base game's workshop incidents include castles.
- War Sails: caravans heading to a castle with a port no longer wait in place when the castle is their best destination.

## 1.1.0

- Castles gain a smithy. **Enter smithy** in the castle menu opens the same smithy screen as in towns, for smithing, smelting and refining with your own materials. Smithing orders stay in towns, so the castle smithy shows none. It has its own MCM switch, **Castle smithy**, on by default.
- The castle menu is laid out like a town menu. **Go to the castle** opens the dungeon, Donate troops to garrison, Manage garrison, Open stash and the lord's hall, as **Go to the keep** does in towns, and Manage castle as well. Each keeps the base game's rules for who sees it. The castle tavern, Recruit troops, Trade and Enter smithy follow in town order. Castles the mod does not cover keep the base game's menu.
- Castle menu changes are registered after the base game finishes creating its options, so **Go to the castle** and its submenu initialize reliably on both new and loaded campaigns.
- Castles open their gates like towns. Visitors the castle would let in walk straight into the castle menu for the tavern, trade, recruits and smithy, without asking the guards. **Go to the castle** now guards the keep as **Go to the keep** does in towns: visitors from outside your kingdom with clan tier below 3 pay the castle's usual bribe, and for them it is greyed out when nobody is inside. The lords in the hall can be spoken to only from inside the castle, not from the castle menu or while waiting. War, any crime rating and a bad relation with the castle's lord still keep you outside, and asking the guards for entry no longer gets around a bad relation. **Request a meeting with someone** stays at the gate. Castles the mod does not cover keep the base game's gate.
- Companions can be hired in castle taverns. On the first eligible day, the active companion system (the base game's, or one from a companion overhaul mod) creates a small, globally capped seed from its unused templates in different castles; a save marker prevents reseeding. If no compatible companion system is active, the seed is skipped once and castle taverns fill only as companions move from towns. Afterward, unhired companions move between towns that are not under siege and nearby castle taverns over time. The seed and new arrivals stop once castles hold a third of the free pool. Moves only go to one of the 3 closest castles or towns, and the **Castle companions** MCM group sets the daily arrival chance (default 100%), daily departure chance (default 10%) and minimum stay between moves (default 7 days, 1 to 30). War Sails' active storyline location model is supported without making the DLC a dependency. The MCM switch, **Companions in castle taverns**, is on by default; turning it off sends tracked unhired castle companions to the nearest reachable town of the castle's culture, or the nearest reachable town if none exists, once they are free to move (not giving a quest, imprisoned, in a party or in the castle you are in), without deleting seeded heroes.
- War Sails caravans can reach castle markets without treating castles as ports. The base game's navigation chooses a land route or a mixed sea-and-land route, including for the northern island castle, while ship services remain exclusive to real ports. Caravans also perform the base game's overweight sell-down when leaving a castle.
- MCM now states the correct requirement for Caravan Ambush: its merchant or artisan must own at least one caravan. Cleanup descriptions now explain the same-culture town preference and that **Keep brothels out** operates independently of the master switch.
- AI lords recruit from castle notables only when **Recruit troops in castles** is enabled, the castle is covered by the mod and that lord's clan is allowed by the two clan-coverage switches. Town and village recruitment remains unchanged.
- Patch diagnostics now name features whose expected game methods are missing even when Harmony skips the patch before applying it.
- Castle merchants get a title after their name, such as "the Broker", as town merchants do. Merchants already in your castles get theirs when you load the save.
- A merchant or artisan who moves from a castle to a town no longer keeps their castle workshop when no notable stays behind to take it over. The workshop is removed instead.
- BLSE is now listed as optional instead of required. This mod does not use it, and none of the mods it needs require it on Steam, GOG or Epic. It is still needed for the Xbox / PC Game Pass version of the game.
- Tested with UIExtenderEx 3.0.3 and MCM 5.13.1.

## 1.0.0

First public version, tested with Bannerlord 1.4.8, War Sails 1.2.8, Harmony 2.4.2 and MCM 5.12.3.

- Castles gain a market, tavern, merchants, artisans, workshops, a Marketplace project, town-style tax and prosperity, caravan trade, recruits and selected quests, each with its own MCM switch.
- Saves that hit the base game's 32 KB save entry limit load again, and the mod splits its own save data so new saves stay under the limit.
- The mod records which taverns, markets, Marketplaces and daily-default buildings it created, and only removes those when it returns a castle to vanilla.
- Castles only link to towns they can reach, including island castles with War Sails.
- A log message names any patch that found nothing to change after a game update or another mod changed the game code.
